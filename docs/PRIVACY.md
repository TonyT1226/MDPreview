# Privacy Policy

[中文](PRIVACY.zh-Hans.md)

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers Markit for macOS, including its Quick Look preview and thumbnail extensions.

## Short version

Markit does not collect any data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it sends nothing to the developer.

## Your documents

- Markit reads the Markdown files you open and writes to them only when you save.
- When you paste or drop an image into the editor, Markit saves a copy in an `assets` folder next to the document.
- Your documents stay on your Mac. Markit does not upload them anywhere.

## Folder access

The Mac App Store version runs in the macOS App Sandbox. To show local images, open linked files or save pasted images, Markit asks you to allow access to the document's folder. It remembers the folders you allow so it doesn't have to ask again. You can see and remove them in Settings › Folder Access.

## Network

Markit connects to the internet only when a document you open contains an image with an `http://` or `https://` address. It then downloads that image directly from the server in the address, the same way a web browser would. That server can see your IP address and the request, as it would for any other visit. Downloaded images are kept in memory and may also be stored in Markit's cache folder on your Mac.

Clicking a web link in a document opens it in your default browser.

The Quick Look extensions do not use the network. They show a placeholder for remote images.

## Settings

Your preferences (font, theme, editor options, allowed folders) are stored on your Mac in Markit's own preferences. They are not synced or sent anywhere.

## Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics for Markit. Apple collects and anonymizes this data under its own privacy policy. Markit itself does not collect it.

## Children

Markit does not collect data from anyone, including children.
